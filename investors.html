Thesis

One shared core, many single-job robots

  • Simple bodies, shared core. Outdoor robots win on cost, reliability and serviceability. One drive-and-power module, one navigation and safety stack and one fleet app serve many simple bodies, each built for one job.
  • First body: a mobile smart bin. It needs no arm or scoop, so the technical risk sits in navigation and safety, which already run in our simulator.
  • Buyers with a budget line. Councils, campuses, estates and venues already pay crews to move bins and pick up litter. Supervised pilots on private and managed land come first.
  • Safety is part of the product. The AI turns requests into structured, checkable jobs and never drives the wheels. A hardware emergency stop, a geofence and walking-pace limits sit underneath.
  • Built lean. A test base in Gulbene, Latvia, and early pilot conversations in the UK and Latvia.
