Software-first outdoor autonomous chore robots

What it is

Wheelbarrow-scale. Wheeled. Swappable. Software-led.

ChoreBot is an outdoor utility platform sized for parks, estates, and municipal grounds — with a flexible bed, swappable attachments, and an LLM-driven chore layer that composes tasks across recurring outdoor work.

  • Platform, not a single chore

    One wheeled base; interchangeable tools for litter, light yard work, and park maintenance.

  • LLM task layer

    Natural-language chore composition on top of autonomy — flexible jobs without a custom planner per attachment.

  • Fleet & ops software

    Scheduling, monitoring, and multi-unit control designed for teams that already run outdoor estate work.

  • Early demos, carefully framed

    Early supervised demos include yard waste pickup on private grounds — one proof chore among outdoor municipal and property jobs, not the whole story.

Why software-first

Commodity chassis. Differentiated stack.

Outdoor utility hardware at this scale is becoming commodity. LOR Robotics bets on the autonomy stack, LLM task layer, and fleet software that turn interchangeable outdoor platforms into multi-chore machines — attach proven chassis, do not reinvent them.

Hardware follows software: prove value in simulation and ops software first, then field hardware to validate the stack with councils and property operators.

  1. 01 Software & sim prove the chore layer
  2. 02 Attach commodity or OEM wheeled chassis
  3. 03 Trial with municipal and property partners
